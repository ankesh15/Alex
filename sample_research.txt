Alex Research Brief:
The Transformer model introduced in 2017 revolutionized natural language processing by replacing recurrent neural networks with self-attention mechanisms. Self-attention calculates relationships between all words in a sequence simultaneously, significantly improving parallel processing capabilities during training. Vector databases such as PostgreSQL with pgvector enable efficient cosine distance indexing of dense semantic embeddings, allowing modern RAG pipelines to retrieve precise document passages and eliminate factual hallucinations in AI-generated answers.
